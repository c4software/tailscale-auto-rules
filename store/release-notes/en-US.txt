First public release.

• Turns the Tailscale tunnel on or off automatically, network by network.
• Network preferences: mark a network as trusted (tunnel off) or as always on.
• Learns the gestures you make in Tailscale and replays them on the same network.
• Airplane mode, mobile data and Wi-Fi rules, each decision explained.
• History of the last 500 changes, with the rule behind each one.
• No account, no server, no analytics: everything stays on the phone.
